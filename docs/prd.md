PRD: Pokémon Monopoly – "The Master League"

Project Code Name: Project Poké-Poly

Version: 1.2

Status: Feature Complete (Rules/Logic)
1. The Procedural Randomizer Tool

The core of this game is the Universal Pokédex Randomizer.
1.1 Generation Logic

When building a board, the tool draws from all 1,000+ Pokémon across all generations. It assigns them to color groups based on Base Stat Totals (BST) to ensure game balance:
Color Tier	BST Range	Commonality	Examples
Brown / Light Blue	150–300	Very Common	Magikarp, Caterpie, Pidgey
Pink / Orange	301–420	Common	Pikachu, Growlithe, Machoke
Red / Yellow	421–520	Rare	Arcanine, Gengar, Lapras
Green / Dark Blue	521–700+	Ultra Rare / Legendary	Tyranitar, Mewtwo, Rayquaza
1.2 User Inputs

    "The Favorite Factor": If a user provides a list of favorite Pokémon, the tool locks them into their respective tier and randomizes the surrounding slots.

    "Regional Lock": Can be toggled to only include Pokémon from a specific region (e.g., "Kanto Only").

2. Item Bag Mechanics (Community Chest)

The Item Bag replaces the Community Chest. These cards represent consumable items or found treasures.
Sample Item Bag Cards:

    Full Restore: "Heal your team." Get out of Team Rocket Hideout (Jail) free.

    Rare Candy: "Boost your Pokémon’s level." Add 1 Berry (House) to any property you own for free.

    Amulet Coin: "Double your prize money." Collect ₽200 from the bank.

    Nugget: "You found a shiny treasure!" Collect ₽100.

    Escape Rope: "Teleport to safety." Move to the nearest "GO" space.

    Leftovers: "Passive healing." For every property you own, collect ₽10 from each player.

3. Team Rocket System

Team Rocket serves as the game’s primary "Tax" and "Jail" mechanic, acting as the antagonistic force that drains Trainer resources.
3.1 The Hideout (Jail)

    Space: "Team Rocket Hideout."

    Mechanic: You are trapped here and cannot collect Battle Fees. To escape, you must pay a ₽50 bribe, roll doubles, or use a "Full Restore" card.

3.2 Team Rocket Grunt (Income Tax)

    Space: "Grunt Ambush."

    Effect: Pay ₽200. Team Rocket has stolen your coins to fund their experiments.

3.3 The Boss: Giovanni (Luxury Tax)

    Space: "Giovanni’s Office."

    Effect: Pay ₽100 or 10% of your total net worth (whichever is higher). Giovanni demands a "protection fee" for your high-level Pokémon.

4. Professor Oak Deck (Chance)

Professor Oak cards focus on the Research and Movement of the Trainer.
Card Title	Description
New Research Site	Advance to the nearest Gym (Railroad).
Wild Encounter	Move forward to the next unowned property. You may buy it or auction it.
Evolution Stone	"Your Pokémon is ready to evolve!" Immediately upgrade 4 Berries into an Evolution Stone (Hotel).
Teleport (Abra)	Move to any space on the board. Do not pass GO.
5. Winning the Game

    Standard: Be the last Trainer with Poké Coins.

    League Champion: Be the first to own 3 complete Type Sets and land on "GO" to enter the Hall of Fame.
